How this page works & how the numbers are calculated

Each row is one daily-wage employee for {{ wage_month|date:"F Y" }}. Leave an input blank to use the automatic value (shown as “auto …” under the box). Type a value only to override it for this month. Click Save Wage Register to store all rows together.

Rate — the employee’s daily rate from their salary record (or monthly basic ÷ days in the month).

Days worked — days marked Present in attendance for the month.

OT (hrs) — time worked beyond the shift length on present days, converted to days using the shift length (8 h if no shift).

Nat’l holidays — pre-filled from your mandatory holidays in the holiday calendar for this month.

Total days = national holidays + days worked + OT days. Wages = rate × total days.

Bonus — 8.33% of the monthly wage (capped at ₹21,000), pro-rated by days worked. Gross = wages + bonus.

EPF — 12% of earnings for days worked (capped at ₹15,000 up to Sep 2026, ₹25,000 from Oct 2026). ESIC — 0.75% of the same, only when earnings are ₹21,000 or less.

Net = gross − EPF − ESIC. Calculated columns refresh after you save.
